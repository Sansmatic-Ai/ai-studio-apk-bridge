# Privacy

AI Studio APK Bridge runs only on `aistudio.google.com` and `ai.studio`.

- It does **not** collect, store, or transmit any personal data.
- It makes **no network requests** of its own (no analytics, no telemetry, no
  remote code).
- The APK captured from AI Studio stays in your browser's memory until you
  download it or close the tab.
- It requests **no extension permissions** beyond running on the two sites above.

You can verify this by reading the source: it is small and unminified.
